# Blueprint: ASCII Aurora

Catalog blueprint: Static site (static-site)

## Idea
A single static page that renders an animated aurora borealis entirely in ASCII characters: undulating teal, green and cyan ribbons with vertical rays over a star field, a dark treeline at the horizon and a lake below reflecting the sky with shimmer. A live HUD shows fps, letters rendered, aurora activity and elapsed time. An original ambient soundtrack is synthesised in the browser with WebAudio and starts only from a user toggle. Inspired by the ASCII aurora renders of dwcminh.ascii. Built by the ANVIL factory line; deployment is out of scope until the human gate.
